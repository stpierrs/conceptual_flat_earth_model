Privacy Policy — Shane's Model 2.0

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Shane's Model 2.0 does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

How the app works

All computation runs entirely on your device. No network requests are made during normal use. The app does not require an internet connection after the initial download.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other device resource.

Third-party services

None. There are no advertising networks, analytics services, crash reporting services, or any other third-party integrations.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new date.
